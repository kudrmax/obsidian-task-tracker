# Task Calendar — plugin vs iPhone app: functional differences

Read on master 4b74f0bf. Sources: docs/plugin/*.md, obsidian/ui, core/application, apple/App, apple/Core/Sources.
Paths: P = obsidian/ui, A = apple/App/Board, C = apple/Core/Sources/Core.
Severity: MISSING (feature absent), WRONG (behaves differently / bug), LOOK (only appearance).
Fix: CORE (the rule/data must come from core/application or the door first), SWIFT (Swift alone).
KNOWN = already named by the lead.

## Decisions

1. **The project's view lives in the app** (4.5, 1.6). In the plugin the group's arrow and a card's folder mark open the project's note, whose end shows the project's block. The app can't show a note, so both open the block itself: the project's view over the screen (full-screen cover, the same ribbon, tabs and plus, every list only the project's tasks, a task written there gets the project). Its bar: Close, Undo, the project's name with a folder, and «⋯» with Search (only the project's tasks), Select Tasks and Open in Obsidian, which opens the note itself. It opens on Inbox the first time and remembers its place by the note.
2. **A dragged card lifts as the capsule itself** (1.8). iOS lets a menu give way to a drag only when the lifted view is the menu's own preview; the copy grown to the whole title shows over the menu, and the drag carries the card as it stands.
3. **The tab bar names places, not columns** (3.5). The plugin's wheel has four column icons, so a tap on one already in the window can stay; the app's stock tab bar has the three places, its selected tab is where the ribbon stands, and a tap on a tab goes to that place. Nothing to change.
4. **Group by Project** is the title menu's one row, as a system menu toggle with a folder icon and the check while on; the words in the app's title case.
5. **A note link in a title opens in Obsidian** (through Advanced URI, `obsidian://advanced-uri?vault=…&filepath=<path>`, the path `notes.open` resolves; Decision 33); an address opens in the system browser.
6. **The Done column closes** when the ribbon comes to rest anywhere off it (a swipe or a tab), as the plugin's «уводят с неё на Inbox».
7. **What the plugin shows between a card and its menu is the menu's first rows** (2.3, 2.4). iOS draws nothing over a menu's preview that takes a tap, and the menu at the tap's point may cover the card. So the description's start is the menu's first row (its first lines as they read, links by their words, no markup), a tap opening the full screen, with a row under it for each link of the description, a tap opening that link; and a calendar card's open subtasks are the next rows, each opening its own menu as a submenu.
8. **A weekly schedule on the Repeat row** (2.6): a system row's value is plain text, so the week's seven letters can't be lit; the row shows the week's letters for the rule's days and a dash for the others (`M – W – F – –`), the seven places reading as the week.
9. **The stretch dots stay after the menu** (5.3). In the plugin the dots ride the copy over the menu and a dot taken closes it; iOS lets nothing under an open menu be dragged. A tapped calendar card stays selected (ringed, its two dots) once its menu has gone, until a touch elsewhere; its dots then stretch it as the plugin's do.
10. **Add Subtask on a calendar card lifts it over a dimmed screen** (2.5): the copy stands where the card stood (kept above the keyboard), its subtasks one level in under it with their branches and the field last, as the plugin's stack under the lifted copy; it goes when typing ends.
11. **A column's title as a drop place lights as an empty section's plate** (5.1): the accent's outline and fill round the title; only cards land there, never the plus.
12. **The Repeat sheet has a check, no cross** (2.7): it writes as it closes however it closes (the check or a swipe down), as the plugin's page writes when its menu closes.
13. **Picked cards are the same card control as everywhere** (5.4): in Select Tasks a tap on a card picks it (no menu), and a picked card's long press lifts the stack: the card on two layers with the number carried on its corner, as the plugin's floating stack.
14. **A find is its card** (1.9, 9.x): the card the lists draw, the found pieces on the selection's ground in semibold (`mark.tc-hit`), the project's name or the description's line under the title in the small muted type, no icon of its own (the card's own description mark stays), as the plugin's search rows; the groups are the system list's sections named by the lists.
15. **The full screen's bar** (11.3, 11.7): Close (the cross) and Undo stand where the main screen has Undo, in one glass group; «+» and «⋯» stay on the right, the check while typing. No system back arrow and no edge swipe: the plugin's only ways back are Close and the parent line.
16. **The description as it reads** (11.5): until a tap types in it, the description is drawn as Obsidian shows it (headings, list items with bullets and depth, quotes, bold and italic, links in the accent opening at a tap); a tap elsewhere puts the caret at its end in the system editor. Embedded images are left out (the app reads no attachments). An empty one (the template's lone list mark) shows the word Description in the muted colour.
17. **The circle stands before the subtask's card** (11.1), as Select Tasks' circle before a card: empty while open, the accent's filled check once done, its cross once dropped; the card stays where it was, crossed out.
18. **Select Tasks' bar** (16.2, 16.3): in place of Undo and «⋯» a glass capsule with the cross and the number picked, the number opening the bulk menu; in place of the plus a glass circle with «⋯» opening the same menu, dimmed while none is picked; the menu keeps the core's order wherever it opens (`menuOrder(.fixed)`), so Delete stays last over the bottom bar. No Select All.
19. **Settings' new rows** (12.x): Cards is a section of the main settings (the plugin's main screen has it) with two system sliders written as the finger lifts; Several labels on Labels; Long press delay and the zoom levels on Day and Timeline; the line markers and Project tag on Tasks File; Google Calendar's client and interval and the work calendar's path on Calendars; each text row written when it is left (Return, or the keyboard's Done for a number). The six preset circles stand before the system colour picker.
20. **A section's header** (3.1, 16.6): a tap opens its menu with nothing lifted over it (the header stays, as a bar button under its menu); a long press lifts the section as the plugin's floating section.
21. **The Done column's tab** (9.2): while the Done column shows, the tab bar names Inbox, the place beside it.
22. **The bulk menu's flags and tags** (10.x): drawn in their level's or label's colour, as the card's menu draws them: the symbol rendered in the core's ink (`rules.inkOf`) for the theme, kept as drawn.
23. **What counts as the same task is the core's** (review; since the merge of master's motion, decision 34): `TaskItem.id`, master's id that every write keeps, comes with every task; the app keys cards, keeps Select Tasks' picks through a label, follows the open menu, the lifted calendar card and the full screen by it, finds the carried group's tasks by the keys `tasks.carried` answers, and builds no identity of its own from a key's fields.
24. **Project is one of the menu's edits** (review): the core's `edits` put `"project"` right after Section (or where it would stand), before Add subtask, in one task's and the bulk menu; `MenuProject.noneLabel` (master's name) is No project's row. Both frontends draw the edits in that order; neither places the row nor words No project itself.
25. **A card is painted in at most four colours, the core's cut** (review): `TaskItem.colors` holds at most `MOST_CARD_COLORS` (4); the plugin keeps one stylesheet slot per colour (a test ties the two), the app paints what it is given.
26. **The `[[` list shows every note the core offers** (review): `notes.suggest`'s 20, scrolling over the bar past five and a half rows; the app cuts none.
27. **The menu's description start is the description** (review): the plugin's peek has no set number of lines (it is cut by its height), so the app hands the whole description to its first row and the iOS menu cuts it (two lines and an ellipsis); there is no line count to take from the core.
28. **The divider holds to the middle by the core's rule** (review): `rules.dividerAt(wanted, widths, speed)` — within 8 of the middle, slower than 0.08 per ms — in both frontends; the app's handle gives a light tap as it comes to hold, as the plugin's haptics do.
29. **The app's Account row says whether Google is logged in** (review): `SettingsForm.googleLoggedIn` (the shared settings keep its token): «Logged In» or «Not Logged In»; logging in stays in Obsidian. The sources' Google row says «not logged in» whenever there is no token, on both platforms, instead of «file missing».
30. **The cards follow Settings → Cards at once** (review): the app's card size is observed (`CardSize`), so every card drawn takes a new height or gap without a relaunch.
31. **A rule of every few units written without `from` reads from the task's own day** (bug 12): the plugin always writes `from`; a hand-written `🔁 every 2 months on the 5th` on a dated task reads as anchored at its due date (or its slot's day) and gets its `from` on the next write; on a task without a day it stays text. Every rule the repeat page can make reads back the same (`tests/domain/repeatRoundTrip.test.ts`).
32. **Plugin fixes found while checking** (bugs 9, 10, 13): the carried card stays inside the window as the menu's copy does (`grownStart`); the desktop quick entry's place button is as wide as its words (80 to 180 px, a longer name fades); a settings page closes with the settings and when the plugin unloads.
33. **Projects as master built them** (merge of master 0.4.180): the app's projects are master's mechanism, parity-app's own copy dropped. The project notes come from `rules.noteTags` and `rules.projectNotes` through `VaultProjects` (a scan of the vault's markdown notes, tags kept on the device, NFC paths, iCloud-only notes counted not read, rescanned every few seconds, the screen redrawn when the list changes); parity-app's `notes.tags` and `notes.projects` door calls, its `NoteIndex` and `TaskBoard.projectNotes()` are gone, and `notes.suggest`/`notes.open` go over a plain list of the vault's files (`Vault.vaultFiles`, NFC). The grouping is kept in a `DeviceText` and changed by `TaskBoard.group(...)`; the lists draw master's `BoardProject` parts and `ProjectHeader`; the title as a drop place, the Project row's page (No project in its own group, `noneLabel`), the folder mark as its own button and the Projects settings page are master's. Kept from parity-app on top: the project's view (Decision 1) — the group's arrow and the folder mark open it, not the note; its Open in Obsidian, a title's note link and the arrow's note all open through Advanced URI, master's one way to open a note (Decision 5 changed); `"project"` placed by the core's `edits` (24); a picked stack dropped on a title; the find drawn as its card (14); the day column grouped in a project's view (`screen.projects.now`); the Automatically update internal links hint on the Projects page.
34. **Motion as master built it** (merge of master 0.4.181): a task's id is master's (`TaskItem.id`, kept by every write while the board hands the door's `ids` back); parity's `keyId`-based id, `TaskItem.place` and `rules.placeId` are gone, and with them the app's own card identity by line and the re-pick after a label. The carry ends through master's one owner (`CarryEnd`): drops fly where `tasks.landing` says, a picked group's copy flying where the lifted task lands; the screen's motion follows what changed (`BoardSnapshot.motion`), never who asked. Kept from parity on top: the section header's UIKit lift and tap menu (`SectionHeaderControl`, Decisions 20), the menu's grown copy, links and the subtasks' rows on a calendar card's menu, the picked stack, the edge scroll, the stretch dots, the Done column, the clock. The ribbon's columns take the divider's widths (master) and a swipe settles on the nearest place (3.6); the Done column is as wide as Inbox. Won't Do and Delete ask on a page of the menu (master), so the confirmation dialogs are gone.
